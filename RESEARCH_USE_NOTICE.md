# Research-use notice

HistoNexa-MIP is provided for research evaluation and reproducibility review.
It is not a cleared medical device, does not replace review by a qualified
pathologist, and must not be used as the sole basis for diagnosis, prognosis,
staging, surgery, or treatment.

The full reviewer package is intended to run on the reviewer's own computer at
`127.0.0.1`. It must not be exposed to a public network without a separate
security, privacy, governance, and authorization review.

The package contains trained model parameters derived from the documented
research pipeline. Those checkpoints incorporate a fine-tuned Phikon backbone
developed by Owkin. Phikon is distributed under the Owkin non-commercial
license, which limits use and sharing to non-commercial research by non-profit
entities. See `THIRD_PARTY_MODEL_NOTICE.md` for attribution, modification, and
license details. Redistribution is permitted only when the authors' institution
has confirmed that the release complies with the Owkin license and all other
applicable institutional and publication terms.

No pathology image, pilot database, physician record, credential, or retained
patient-related upload is included in the package.
